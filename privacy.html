Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes how the Pocket Stereo iOS app ("the app", "we") handles information. We designed the app to work on your devices without creating an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to our servers. The app has no user accounts.

Camera, microphone, and photos

  • Camera: used to record video for stereo capture and for calibration.
  • Microphone: used to record audio when audio recording is enabled.
  • Photos: used to save your exported videos to your Photos library when you choose to export.

Recordings and calibration data stay on your devices unless you choose to share or export them.

Local network and device-to-device transfer

To pair two iPhones, the app uses your local Wi-Fi network (Local Network permission) to discover the other device, synchronize recording clocks, and transfer footage directly between your two devices. This transfer does not pass through our servers.

Purchases

Pocket Stereo Pro is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. The app checks your purchase status through Apple's StoreKit.

Third parties, analytics, and advertising

The app does not include advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.

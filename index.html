Two iPhones. One 3D camera.

Pocket Stereo synchronizes two iPhones to record paired stereo video, ready for VR headsets, 3D displays, and spatial video players.

Download on the App Store

How it works

One iPhone is the Controller, the other is the Companion. They find each other over Wi-Fi, synchronize their recording clocks, and start and stop together. Afterward the app transfers the footage and composites it into a side-by-side stereo video, with optional rectification to align the two cameras.

Features

  • Synchronized recording across two iPhones
  • Stereo calibration with a ChArUco board, shown on any screen or printed
  • Optical rectification removes lens distortion and aligns both cameras
  • 1080p or 4K at up to 60fps, HEVC or H.264 output
  • Save takes and reprocess any time, even after recalibrating
  • One Pro purchase unlocks both phones in a recording pair

Pocket Stereo Pro

Upgrade once to remove the watermark and record without time limits. If either phone in a connected pair has Pro, both run unlocked for that session.

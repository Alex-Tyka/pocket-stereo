Frequently asked questions

The two iPhones can't find each other.

  • Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Local Network access for Pocket Stereo on both phones (Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network).
  • Force-quit and reopen the app on both phones, then try again.

How do I calibrate the cameras?

Display the ChArUco calibration board on a screen or print it, mount the two phones side by side, and follow the on-screen steps in the app. Recalibrating later lets you reprocess saved takes.

How should I mount the two phones?

Mount them side by side, facing the same direction, and keep them from shifting between calibration and recording. If the phones move, recalibrate.

What does Pocket Stereo Pro include?

Pro removes the watermark and the clip time limit. If either phone in a connected pair has Pro, both are unlocked for that session.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my recordings saved?

Takes are stored on your devices. Exported videos are saved to your Photos library or wherever you choose to share them.
